Behind the scene
After ordering - Step 1
We’ve received your order and start the printing procress
This process usually takes a few days, maybe weeks depending on the nature of the order, the amount of item we have to process and other factors such as equipment, weather and materials availability. But keep in mind that we’re always trying our best to finish preparing your product as soon as we can!
Delivery - Step 2
Once we finished preparing your orders, we’ll ship it out for you!
Depending on your request and the amount of item, we either ship your order to you using cars, trucks or simply through postal services. For international customers we’ll do the heavy lifting of getting it through custom and ship it to your country where our partners will pick it up and deliver it straight to your doorstep!
If anything happened during this step you’ll receive information about it through the Email you provided us!
post-delivery - Step 3
You have received your orders
After we finished delivering it to you, we’ll simply send you an Email confirming that you have indeed received your orders. The last step is you enjoying the product you wanted
